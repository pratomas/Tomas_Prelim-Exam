# Activity 7 – AI-Generated Resume Comparison

## Activity Description

This activity involved using two artificial intelligence tools, ChatGPT and Claude AI, to generate professional nursing resumes. Both tools were used to create a one-page resume for an entry-level Staff Nurse position at Southern Philippines Medical Center (SPMC). The generated outputs were then compared based on their content, organization, visual presentation, and readability.

## Comparative Evaluation

The ChatGPT-generated resume featured a minimalist layout with concise content and a simple visual presentation. Meanwhile, the Claude AI-generated resume provided more distinct section divisions, clearer typography, and a more organized arrangement of information. Both outputs demonstrated how AI tools can assist in creating professional resumes, although their formatting and presentation differed.

## Learning Reflection

This activity helped me recognize how different AI tools interpret the same instructions and produce different results. I learned that the quality of an AI-generated resume depends not only on the tool used but also on the clarity and completeness of the instructions. One challenge was evaluating which resume presented the information more effectively; I addressed this by comparing their readability, organization, and overall professional appearance. In the future, I can use AI tools as writing and design assistants while reviewing and editing their outputs to ensure accuracy and suitability for professional applications.
