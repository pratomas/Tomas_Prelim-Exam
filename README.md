## 💡 Part B — Mini Project Documentation

📄 **File:** `Project Documentation_TOMAS.pdf`

🔗 **View File:** [Project Documentation_TOMAS.pdf](https://github.com/pratomas/Tomas_Prelim-Exam/blob/main/Activity%203/Project%20Documentation_TOMAS.pdf)

### Project Title

**Addressing Sudden Flooding in Communities through an Automated Flood Monitoring and Early Warning System**

### Project Overview

The mini project explores how **Information Technology can be used to improve flood preparedness and community safety**.

The proposed system uses automated water-level sensors, data collection, and mobile/web notifications to provide communities with timely information about rising water levels.

### Proposed Solution

The **Smart Flood Alert and Monitoring System** would:

- Continuously monitor water levels
- Automatically detect flood-level changes
- Classify conditions as Normal, Alert, Warning, or Critical
- Provide real-time information through a monitoring platform
- Send automated warnings and emergency notifications
- Provide location-based flood information
- Store historical water-level records
- Provide evacuation and emergency preparedness reminders
- Display a simple flood-risk map

### Target Users

The proposed system is intended for:

- Residents
- Barangay officials
- Disaster response teams
- Schools
- Businesses
- Organizations in vulnerable communities

### Expected Impact

The system aims to help communities:

- Evacuate earlier
- Avoid dangerous roads and flood-prone areas
- Protect belongings and property
- Improve disaster response coordination
- Make faster decisions using real-time information
- Improve community preparedness
- Reduce delays in detecting sudden flooding

# 🎯 Final Reflection

## What is the Importance of Presentation Design?

Presentation design is important because it helps transform information into something that is easier to understand, remember, and engage with.

A well-designed presentation considers the **audience, purpose, visual hierarchy, readability, color, typography, and organization of information**.

Through the activities in this portfolio, I learned that effective design is not simply about making something visually appealing. It is about creating a meaningful visual experience that allows information to be communicated clearly and professionally.

As a nursing student, these skills can also be applied beyond academic requirements. Clear presentations, informative infographics, and well-organized digital materials can help communicate health information more effectively to patients, communities, classmates, and future colleagues.

> **Good design does not only make information look better — it helps people understand it better.**
