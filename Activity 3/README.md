# Activity 3: Social Media Infographic and Mini Project Documentation

## Activity Description

This activity focused on applying digital design and information technology concepts through the creation of a social media infographic and a mini project proposal. The infographic discussed sudden flooding, including its causes, warning signs, and safety measures. The mini project explored the use of an automated flood monitoring and early warning system to help improve community preparedness and disaster response.

## Activity Outputs

### Part A: Social Media Infographic

**Topic: Sudden Flooding**

The infographic presents important information about sudden flooding in a visually organized and easy-to-understand format.

It highlights the following:

- Causes of sudden flooding
- Warning signs and indicators
- Safety measures and preparedness
- The importance of staying informed during emergencies

**[View Social Media Infographic PDF](Infographic_TOMAS.pdf)**

### Part B: Mini Project Documentation

**Project Title: Addressing Sudden Flooding in Communities through an Automated Flood Monitoring and Early Warning System**

The proposed project explores how technology can support flood monitoring and provide timely warnings to communities at risk.

The proposed system includes:

- Automated water-level monitoring
- Detection of changes in water levels
- Flood-level alerts and notifications
- Real-time monitoring and information
- Emergency preparedness reminders

## What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned how visual communication can make important information easier to understand and share with the public. I also gained an appreciation for how information technology can be applied to real-world community problems, particularly in disaster preparedness and flood monitoring. One challenge I encountered was organizing technical information into a format that would be understandable and useful to the intended audience. I addressed this by arranging the information into clear sections and identifying the most relevant points for the infographic and proposed system. In the future, I can apply these skills when developing informative digital materials and exploring technology-based solutions to community and healthcare-related concerns.
